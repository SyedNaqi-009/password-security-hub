Published: Sept 2026 • By PSH Security Labs • 7 Min Read

Year after year, annual security threat reports reveal the same troubling reality: passwords like 123456, admin, and password remain the most frequently chosen credentials on the internet.

The Danger of "L33t Speak" Substitutions

Many users believe changing password into P@$$w0rd1! makes them safe. Modern dictionary cracking tools like Hashcat and John the Ripper use rule-based engines specifically programmed to test every leet-speak variation in less than a second. True security requires genuine randomness, not predictable character swaps.
