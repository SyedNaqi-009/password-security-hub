Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

1. Introduction & Core Commitment

At Password Security Hub ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible from https://passwordsecurityhub.com, user privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy document describes how information is handled when you visit our website.

2. Zero Collection of Password & Credential Data

We do not collect, process, record, or transmit any passwords, passphrases, PINs, usernames, hashes, or security codes generated or checked on our platform. All calculations, random number generation, and cryptographic hashing take place 100% locally on your device within your browser using the client-side Web Crypto API.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like most websites, we may utilize standard web cookies for basic site preferences (such as remembering your Dark Mode selection) and aggregate traffic analytics. These cookies contain no personally identifiable information.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. GDPR and CCPA Rights

Under European GDPR and California CCPA privacy regulations, consumers have rights regarding their personal data. Because Password Security Hub does not collect personal identity or account information, there is no personal database to access, rectify, or delete.

6. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@passwordsecurityhub.com or via our Contact Form.
