Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

Introduction & Core Commitment

Welcome to Password Security Hub. Your privacy is not just a priority for us; it is the foundational principle of our platform. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices, explaining how our service works and assuring you of our zero data collection policy regarding your sensitive security information.

Core Commitment: We do not, under any circumstances, collect, store, transmit, or monitor any passwords, passphrases, hashes, or security inputs you generate or evaluate using our tools.

100% Client-Side Processing Architecture

To guarantee the security of your data, Password Security Hub relies entirely on client-side processing. When you visit our site, the necessary scripts (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) are downloaded to your browser. All computational tasks, including random number generation and password entropy analysis, are performed locally on your device by your browser.

We utilize the browser's native Web Crypto API to ensure cryptographically secure operations without making any requests to a backend server. Once loaded, our core tools are fully functional offline.

Cookies and Analytics

While we strictly avoid collecting sensitive inputs, our website may use standard web technologies like cookies to ensure functionality and understand general site usage.

  • Essential Cookies: Used to remember user preferences, such as your selected theme (light or dark mode).
  • Google Analytics 4: We use GA4 to understand how users interact with our site (e.g., page views, session duration) to improve user experience. This data is aggregated and anonymized.

Advertising Policies

To keep our tools free, we use third-party advertising networks, such as Google AdSense. These vendors may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party services (e.g., password managers, security hardware). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to read their respective privacy policies.

Data Retention

Because we do not collect any user security credentials or personal identifying information (aside from standard server logs like IP addresses for security and DDoS mitigation, kept for a maximum of 30 days), we have zero retention of your passwords or passphrases.

User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have rights regarding their personal data. Given our architecture, we hold no personal data directly linked to you, barring any aggregated analytics or cookie data collected by third parties.

If you wish to exercise your rights regarding cookies or analytics, you may use browser settings or the opt-out links provided above.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under the age of 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Changes to This Policy & Contact Details

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, please contact us at: support@passwordsecurityhub.com or via our Contact Page.
