How to Use This Tool

  1. Select your desired password length using the slider. 16+ is recommended.
  2. Choose which character types to include: uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
  3. Enable 'Exclude Ambiguous' or add custom excludes to tailor your password.
  4. Click 'Generate Password' and 'Copy' to use it in your password manager.

Why Use Our Password Generator

Creating strong, unique passwords for every account is the most effective way to protect yourself online. Human-created passwords are often predictable, relying on common words, dates, or keyboard patterns. Attackers use sophisticated software capable of guessing billions of passwords per second.

Our tool uses cryptographic client-side algorithms to ensure maximum randomness (entropy). This means your passwords are mathematically complex and virtually immune to brute-force or dictionary attacks. Furthermore, all generation happens on your device—we never see or store your passwords.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this password generator safe to use?

Yes. All password generation happens entirely in your browser using secure client-side algorithms (crypto.getRandomValues). Passwords are never sent to a server.

What is the recommended password length?

For strong security, we recommend a minimum of 16 characters. Longer passwords offer exponentially more security.

Why shouldn't I use words found in the dictionary?

Attackers use dictionary attacks to guess passwords quickly. Randomly generated characters ensure your password isn't in any dictionary.

Can I generate multiple passwords at once?

Yes, use the 'Generate Multiple' toggle to create up to 10 passwords at a single time.

What are ambiguous characters?

Ambiguous characters are letters and numbers that look alike, such as uppercase 'I', lowercase 'l', and the number '1'. Excluding them makes reading and typing the password easier.

How do I remember these passwords?

You shouldn't try to memorize them! We strongly recommend using a reputable password manager to securely store and auto-fill these randomly generated passwords.
