    How Strength Scoring Works

    Unlike simplistic checkers that only count characters or search for symbols, our tool utilizes the industry-standard zxcvbn algorithm developed by Dropbox security teams. It analyzes spatial keyboard patterns (e.g. qwerty), date sequences (e.g. 1998), repeated substrings, and over 30,000 common dictionary words.

    Zero-Risk Password Testing

    Your input is never transmitted across the network. All scoring logic runs purely in your device's memory. You can even disconnect your internet connection to verify complete offline operation.
