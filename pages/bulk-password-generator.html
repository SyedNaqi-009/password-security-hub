How to Use the Bulk Generator

  1. Set Criteria: Choose the desired length and character types for your passwords.
  2. Select Quantity: Use the slider to pick how many passwords you need (up to 500).
  3. Generate: Click the "Generate Passwords" button.
  4. Export: Use the export buttons to download your list as a CSV or text file.

Why Use a Bulk Password Generator?

When provisioning accounts for new employees, managing test environments, or resetting multiple system credentials, generating passwords manually is tedious and often results in weak patterns.

Our bulk generator ensures every password meets high entropy standards and is cryptographically random, saving time for IT administrators while strictly enforcing security best practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many passwords can I generate at once?

You can generate up to 500 passwords at a single time using our bulk generator.

Is it safe to generate passwords in bulk online?

Yes, our tool generates all passwords locally in your web browser. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Can I export the generated passwords?

Yes, you can export the full list as a CSV or TXT file for easy importing into password managers or IT systems.

Are the generated passwords cryptographically secure?

Absolutely. We use the Web Crypto API's crypto.getRandomValues() to ensure high-quality randomness.

What are ambiguous characters?

Ambiguous characters are those that look similar, like uppercase 'I' and lowercase 'l', or the number '0' and letter 'O'.

Can I hide the passwords while generating?

Yes, passwords are obfuscated by default to prevent shoulder surfing. You can reveal them individually or all at once.
