How to Use

Select the number of words you want in your passphrase, choose a separator, and customize the capitalization or number inclusion. Click generate to instantly create a secure passphrase. The passphrase is created locally on your device for maximum privacy and security.

Why Use a Passphrase?

Passphrases are typically longer than conventional passwords, which makes them much harder for attackers to crack (providing higher entropy). Because they are made of real words, they are significantly easier for humans to remember than random strings of characters.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a passphrase?

A passphrase is a sequence of words used to control access to a computer system, program, or data. It offers high security while being memorable.

Is this passphrase generator secure?

Yes. All generation happens locally in your browser using secure cryptographic functions (`crypto.getRandomValues()`). We do not track or store the passphrases generated.
