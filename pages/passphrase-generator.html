Why Passphrases Beat Complex Passwords

The famous XKCD comic "Correct Horse Battery Staple" highlighted a fundamental security truth: long combinations of common words selected randomly from a large dictionary have far higher entropy than short, obfuscated strings like Tr0ub4dor&3, while remaining vastly easier to remember and type on mobile touchscreens.
